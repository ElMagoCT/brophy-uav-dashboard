Proficient Flight
In the sim: orbit, Split-S, gaps, and finish a race.
The standard
A mentor watches you in Liftoff: a clean orbit around a gate, a Split-S, three gaps without a crash, and a finished race.
Practise first in Ground School: Four channels, two sticks · Rate mode against angle mode · Hover Trainer · Throttle discipline

Line of Sight
Without goggles: take off, hover and land a whoop in angle mode. Level flying only, supervised.
The standard
A mentor stands beside you: a controlled take-off to head height, a 20-second hover inside a two-metre box, a slow nose-in turn, and a landing on the pad.
Practise first in Ground School: Which way is left? · Gate Run · Lost orientation recovery
