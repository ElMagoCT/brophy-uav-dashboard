Tier 2 · pt. 1Witnessed
FAA TRUST
Complete the free FAA TRUST certificate online (~20 min, cannot be failed).
The standard
Show your TRUST certificate (PDF or photo) to a mentor. DM it to mtucker27@brophybroncos.org for the records.
Why Federal requirement for recreational flyers. Official.
2
Tier 2 · pt. 2Witnessed
Spotter
Run the pre-flight checklist; keep visual line of sight and call hazards.
The standard
Spot a real flight for a mentor: run the checklist aloud, keep eyes on the aircraft the whole battery, call every person and obstacle before the pilot needs to know.
Why FPV pilots can’t see around themselves.
3
Tier 2 · pt. 3Quiz
Event Ops
Set up a flight zone, brief bystanders, keep flights away from crowds; demonstrate failsafe setup, flyaway response, LiPo fire procedure and incident reporting.
8 lessons, then the quiz
  1. The flight zone
  2. Briefing bystanders
  3. Pilot and spotter call-outs
  4. Failsafe: set it, test it
  5. Flyaway and lost video
  6. LiPo fire and injury
  7. The incident report
  8. Scenario drill drill
10 questions · 80 % passes · no sign-off needed · retake any time
Why Ground work before flying at a game or rally, and a plan for when something goes wrong.
4
Tier 2 · pt. 4 · FilmingWitnessed
Cinematography
Write a shot list for a real event with a coach or moderator; frame a follow shot, an orbit and a reveal; deliver one usable clip.
The standard
A written shot list agreed with the coach or moderator and used on the day, and one clip with a follow, an orbit and a reveal that the editor accepts without re-shooting.
Why Turns pilots into shooters for Best of Brophy who can work with other organizations professionally. Filming.
5
Tier 2 · pt. 5 · RacingWitnessed
Indoor Proximity
Fly a whoop through a hallway or doorway course without touching walls.
The standard
The club hallway course, one battery, zero wall contact, a mentor counting.
Why Precision in tight spaces — what an indoor rally race demands. Racing.
6
Tier 2 · pt. 6 · RacingWitnessed
Racing
3 clean laps of the club course under a target time.
The standard
Three consecutive clean laps under the posted target time, timed by a mentor.
Why Gate discipline under pressure; qualifies a pilot for the rally race. Racing.
