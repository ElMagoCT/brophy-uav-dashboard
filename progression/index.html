How a badge is earned

1 · Show it
Do the skill in front of a certified member — someone who already holds that badge. Demonstration, not attendance.
2 · Sign-off
They sign it off on the club’s tracking board. Examiners hold the Mentor badge plus the badge being tested, with officer approval.
3 · Unlock
Finish every badge in your tier and the gate opens: the next tier’s drones are yours to fly. Electives can be done any time.
