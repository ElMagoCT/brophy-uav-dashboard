How it works

Quiz badges
Short lessons, then a 10-question quiz. 80 % passes. Retake as often as you like; your best score counts. The kiosk awards these by itself.
Bench badges
Step-by-step instructions on the kiosk or here. Tick the boxes as you go, DM a photo of your work to mtucker27@brophybroncos.org, then a mentor signs it off.
Witnessed badges
A mentor who holds the badge watches you do it against the standard written on the card. Prep drills are in Ground School.
Gates
Earn every badge in a tier and the gate opens: the next drone is yours to fly. Bonus badges are extra, any time, and don’t count toward your total — Mentor is for Tier 2 pilots.
